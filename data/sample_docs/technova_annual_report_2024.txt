TechNova Financial Holdings — Annual Report FY 2024

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

TechNova Financial Holdings reported record revenues of $4.82 billion for fiscal year 2024, representing a 17.3% increase year-over-year from $4.11 billion in FY2023. Net income attributable to common shareholders reached $892 million, or $3.47 per diluted share, compared to $721 million, or $2.81 per diluted share in FY2023.

RISK FACTORS

1. Interest Rate Sensitivity: A 100 basis point increase in benchmark interest rates would increase our annual interest expense by approximately $11.2 million based on our current floating-rate debt of $1.12 billion. Approximately 78% of our long-term debt is fixed-rate, mitigating this exposure.

2. Regulatory Risk: The company operates in 34 jurisdictions. Evolving financial data regulations (including EU DORA, SEC data governance rules, and India PDPB) may require significant compliance investments estimated at $45-65 million over FY2025-FY2026.

3. Customer Concentration: Our top 10 customers represent 28.4% of total revenue. Loss of any top-3 customer could reduce annual revenues by $180-$260 million.

4. Cybersecurity Risk: We process approximately 4.2 terabytes of sensitive financial data daily. A material breach could result in regulatory penalties, client attrition, and reputational damage exceeding $500 million in estimated total impact.

5. AI Model Risk: Our AI-powered products use machine learning models trained on historical financial data. Model performance may degrade during unprecedented market conditions, and algorithmic errors could expose clients to financial losses.
